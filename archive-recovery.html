Why this still cannot show what changed after ITV

ITV published its findings on 14 October 2026. The weekly study ends on 15 October. It therefore contains only the following day’s observation, with no later post-investigation weeks. Filling the summer gaps would improve the earlier baseline; it cannot create those later observations.

ITV’s dated investigation

A September investigation and the October ITV investigation must have separate event dates and matched windows. A publication count cannot stand in for homepage prominence.

What would make the comparison stronger?

For the earlier period: a recoverable, timestamped homepage or screenshot within the same matching window. For the October investigation: later observations collected at the same times across all three outlets, with missing captures and placement recorded. Changes would remain an association to examine, rather than proof that an investigation caused editorial behaviour.
