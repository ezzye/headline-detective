Aggregate and method
  • The Arday counts exclude broadcasts, video-only pieces and passing mentions. Qualified indexing means counts can be lower bounds. The earlier tally of 13 mixed pages/programmes is not interchangeable with the nine written pieces here.
  • Eleven Arday examples illustrate coverage. Three BBC headlines and their current article bodies were checked directly on 13 October. Current pages do not establish every original August version. Telegraph examples remain short inventory excerpts.
  • Flack’s 99 are not all established as negative. The online Valentine-card example is reported by another publisher; its membership in the print corpus is unknown. The removal date falls outside the counting window, and its exact timing relative to the public announcement is unresolved.
  • The counting methods differ. Neither missing dates nor missing searches establish zero publication. A changed or removed page alone does not establish a cover-up.
