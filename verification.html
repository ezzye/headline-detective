Evidence boundaries to preserve

  • The six cases use different windows and sources. Their recovered headline examples are finite samples, not exhaustive coverage records.
  • Flack’s 99 is the Guardian-reported aggregate of Sun print stories within a 387-story national-paper mention count. It is not a dated item-level list and does not establish that all 99 stories were negative. Keep it separate from the dated headline sample.
  • Outlets used as fictional role-play desks are not graded. Users’ coverage verdicts, rubric ratings and suggested counts are discussion choices, not scored answers.
  • Only evidence checks may contribute to a quiz score. The exercise cannot establish motive, guilt, discrimination or a causal effect on a person’s health or death.
  • Local image assets need working offline paths and complete attribution. Check dates, author, license, file-page URL and stored-file hash in assets/media-credits.json.

Provider and privacy target

The accepted Apex target is fixed to DeepSeek Flash and optional ElevenLabs Eleven v3. It uses donated prepaid-remaining balances with no extra owner-paid monthly allocation and no pay-as-you-go overage. Service should fail closed when the prepaid remainder or shared safety envelope is exhausted. Keys must stay server-side, and donor revocation must prevent future dispatch of queued work.

This is a target description, not an operational result. The deployed model identifiers, provider account state, prepaid balance, server-side key handling, revocation and usage settlement remain unverified until supported by release evidence. No paid calls were made for this verification receipt.
