Media justice resources

Independent organisations that work on journalism standards, media power, press accountability, hate and racial equality. They have different roles and viewpoints; this directory is a starting point, not an endorsement.

Choose the right route. A press regulator or complaints scheme handles only publishers within its remit. Campaign groups and research organisations offer education, evidence or advocacy; they do not decide individual complaints.
campaign / reform advocacy

Hacked Off

Campaigns for reform of press self-regulation and voice/protection for people affected by press abuse. Its positions are advocacy; distinguish them from regulator or court findings.
