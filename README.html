Faithful to the Facts

A six-case media-literacy game. Read dated public records, examine the value and limits of coverage, and separate sourced facts from your own editorial judgment.

Evidence boundary. Each case uses a fixed, dated research sample. These examples help people practice close reading; they are not an exhaustive census of every outlet, format or article. The cases use different counting rules, so their totals cannot be ranked or compared as if measured the same way.

Six cases

2026 · Jason Arday

A 22-day dated inventory

The selected NewsCord inventory lists 229 substantive written items from 15 outlets. The game’s four Arday desks account for 90 dated items. It excludes passing mentions, broadcasts and video-only pieces; qualified indexing means the total may be a lower bound. Examples are a small selection, not the counting population.

2019–20 · Caroline Flack

Aggregate count beside a dated sample

The Guardian reported 99 print stories in The Sun out of 387 UK national-paper stories mentioning Flack in the headline or body during 14 August 2019–14 February 2020. The 99 are not all established as negative. This aggregate does not provide an item-level census or daily totals; the game’s dated headline examples are a separate illustrative sample.

2018 · Raheem Sterling

Two linked episodes, kept distinct

A bounded sample spans the tattoo coverage in May–June and a later December discussion of racialised portrayals. The episodes are linked by Sterling’s public account of repeated scrutiny, but each has its own events and context. The sample does not measure all reporting about him.

2010–11 · Christopher Jefferies

Arrest, suspicion and later accountability

The dated sample includes eight early print headlines reproduced in Jefferies’s Leveson witness-statement exhibit and later reporting on consequences and reflection. Suspicion was not proof of guilt. The later items are context and accountability examples, not a complete article corpus.

2023 · Nicola Bulley

Search coverage and review of press conduct

A short, bounded sample follows changing public-interest developments during the search and the later review and inquiry announcements. Police disclosures and subsequent press-conduct findings are presented with their dates and sources. It does not reproduce private family material or claim that coverage caused an outcome.

2019 · Meghan Markle

A defined first wave around the letter

An 18-day sample covers public discussion and publication of extracts from a private letter, responses and the first legal developments. Later court findings are marked as retrospective. The sample is not a measure of all coverage of Markle or the Royal Family.

Across the cases, headline counts measure documented attention under their stated rules—not hostility, intent, harm or a causal effect. Missing records and missing searches do not establish that nothing was published.

How to play

  1. Read the case and its counting note. Check the window, unit and exclusions before interpreting any total.
  2. Follow the event timeline. Keep allegations, denials, verified developments and later findings in their dated status.
  3. Apply the same six questions. Consider public value, context, framing, care, consistency and transparency.
  4. Hear a simulated editorial defence. The first-person scripts are fictional, source-linked prompts for discussion. They are not statements from the named outlets.
  5. Make your own judgment. Case verdicts, ratings and suggested counts are ungraded. Points apply only to factual evidence checks.

The learning activity supports media literacy and discussion. It is not legal, clinical or investigative advice, and it does not determine guilt, motive, discrimination or causation.

Local use

  1. Install Node.js 22 or later.
  2. Open a terminal in this project folder and run:
npm test
npm start

Then open http://127.0.0.1:9027/. Static case text and local images are available without provider access. Live questions and voice require the local server and the currently configured provider path.

Do not publish the site or expose a server with provider credentials without an explicit security and deployment review.

Reviewed introductions share recordings across players while their exact spoken text, actor, model, performance and settings remain unchanged. New answers retain private, short-lived recordings. Page memory reuses audio on replay. Fixed recordings are kept as separate encrypted audio files in the private cache; missing or edited recordings require synthesis.
