What the timeline can show

  1. 1 · Replay a day
    Move along the shared date axis. Headlines, contributing posts, pushback and editorials keep separate counts.
  2. 2 · Follow a connection
    Open the source behind a promotion, preview, quote or reply. Same-day ordering stays unknown where timestamps are missing.
  3. 3 · Challenge the outlet
    Ask what changed, what was repeated and why the continued attention served the public. Later aftermath is optional and marked.

We can document sequence and particular sharing links, not reconstruct the whole viral wave. Counters observed on 14 October 2026 are cumulative snapshots. They do not reveal the number of people reached in the original window, a daily growth rate or harm. A historical snapshot is kept separate.

What later scrutiny established

A public-interest defence needs to face the outcomes too. Sixteen dated accountability records now distinguish court findings, review conclusions, announcements and an institution’s own account.

Keep the dates in view. Later findings can test the quality of earlier decisions. They do not prove what an editor knew at publication, supply a verdict on every article or turn an allegation into an established fact at an earlier date. Your judgment about proportionate coverage remains yours.

Jefferies: appearance is not evidence of guilt

Leveson’s sections 4.12–4.21 examine reporting about character, personality and temperament, and criticise the damaging publication choices. The Mirror editor’s claim of off-record police briefings was disputed by the Chief Constable; the inquiry could not resolve the conflict. Its criticism of reckless judgment sits alongside its finding that there was no evidence of Mirror malice.

Xarchive

An independent search interface over Wayback’s public Twitter/X captures. It can help find archived URLs; it is not a separate, complete archive of deleted posts.

How Xarchive works ↗

Contemporary articles with embeds, quoted text or screenshots can corroborate a missing post. Preserve the source, account, exact link and dates, and label what was actually recovered. A screenshot alone does not establish the original post’s reach or authenticity.
