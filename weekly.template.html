What appeared in reviewed captures?

Each count is the number of dates with at least one qualifying item in the defined pool, out of the matched reviewed weeks. Bars show the share of matched dates (full width = all eligible weeks). These are dates, not article totals. A zero is meaningful only when all seven metrics were reviewed.

Separate topic and scrutiny counts

These are separate, overlapping topic and scrutiny measures, not scores. Crime means explicit crime, alleged offending, fraud, smuggling or literal illegality. Removals, interception, deportation and cost rules alone count as migration enforcement, not crime. A story may fit both. Routine policy, appointments and ordinary political responses are coded separately from conduct scrutiny and adverse performance. Harm or protection is coded when death, victimisation, abuse, distress, protection or counter-stigma is foregrounded; restrictions alone do not imply harm.

A repeated Featured investigation counts once on each sampled date it remains visible. That is persistence on the homepage, not several new articles. “Literal illegal” requires that exact word in a qualifying migration headline or visible deck.
