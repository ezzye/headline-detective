Privacy and shared usage

This is a public educational game. No account is required. Updated 15 October 2026.

When you play

Reading the record and making a judgment need no paid AI request. Pressing Ask sends your question, the relevant public case record and up to three earlier answers from that outlet to DeepSeek. For dynamic outlet replies, pressing Play voice sends the approved reply or reviewed script to ElevenLabs when a saved recording is unavailable. The short challenge uses prepared, cached ElevenLabs recordings: after you enter the game, its host welcomes you and reads questions and feedback automatically. Host on / Mute stops current and pending host narration. No host audio starts on page arrival, and playing these prepared recordings makes no new provider request. Avoid entering private information about yourself or others.

Encrypted game sessions and audio for individual questions expire after 20 minutes. Reviewed introductions use shared recordings while their text, actor, model and delivery settings stay unchanged. Those fixed recordings contain only the project’s public scripts and are kept in a bounded cache until replaced or evicted. Expired entries are removed when the gateway next runs. The site stores a private, irreversible keyed hash of your network address for daily usage protection; it does not store the raw address in its game ledger. Hosting providers handle network requests under their own policies. The game does not run advertising or audience analytics scripts.

Your browser may remember earned evidence badges on this device. It does not store provider keys, questions or withdrawal codes in local storage. Audio replay buffers stay only in page memory for up to 20 minutes; closing the page clears them.

When you watch the film

The home page shows a locally hosted poster. The YouTube player loads only after you press Play; the page does not contact YouTube for this film before that choice. Once loaded, the player connects to YouTube, which handles viewing data under its own policies and may show advertising. We use YouTube’s privacy-enhanced embed mode. You can skip the film and play the game.

The full-song link opens SoundCloud in a new tab. About YouTube embedded players · Google privacy policy

When you contribute a key

The password field sends your key to this site’s protected API. It is encrypted in private AWS storage and used only for the provider and model you approve. Other visitors never receive it. Contributions have a finite usage allowance, a call ceiling and an expiry, and do not renew automatically. Source code on GitHub contains no working keys.

Keep the private withdrawal code. Revoking stops new calls; a request already sent may finish. Conservative reservations can consume an allowance when a provider’s outcome is uncertain. The ledger counts exact spoken characters for ElevenLabs and validated token usage for DeepSeek; it does not convert money into tokens or purchase credit.

The site checks provider funding where available and declines requests that exceed known credits. Your provider account settings remain yours to control. Use a dedicated key and provider-side restrictions where available.

Storage and maintenance

Keys are held in a server secret store or encrypted donor vault. Session and usage snapshots are encrypted, with private older snapshot versions retained for up to 14 days for recovery. Withdrawal disables a donated credential; encrypted accounting records remain for usage protection. Provider services have their own retention policies.

The project owner updates remaining allowances and provider availability during maintenance. Shared funding supports answers and voices; it buys no influence over case evidence or verdicts. For a project issue, use the public repository without including keys or private withdrawal codes.
