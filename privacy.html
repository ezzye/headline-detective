Privacy and shared usage

This is a public educational game. No account is required. Updated 13 October 2026.

When you play

Reading the record and making a judgment need no paid AI request. Pressing Ask sends your question, the relevant public case record and up to three earlier answers from that outlet to DeepSeek. Pressing Play voice sends the approved reply or reviewed script to ElevenLabs. Avoid entering private information about yourself or others.

Encrypted game sessions and reusable audio expire after 20 minutes. Expired entries are removed when the gateway next runs. The site stores a private, irreversible keyed hash of your network address for daily usage protection; it does not store the raw address in its game ledger. Hosting providers handle network requests under their own policies. There are no advertising or audience analytics scripts.

Your browser may remember earned evidence badges on this device. It does not store provider keys, questions or withdrawal codes in local storage.

When you contribute a key

The password field sends your key to this site’s protected API. It is encrypted in private AWS storage and used only for the provider and model you approve. Other visitors never receive it. Contributions have a finite usage allowance, a call ceiling and an expiry, and do not renew automatically. Source code on GitHub contains no working keys.

Keep the private withdrawal code. Revoking stops new calls; a request already sent may finish. Conservative reservations can consume an allowance when a provider’s outcome is uncertain. The ledger counts exact spoken characters for ElevenLabs and validated token usage for DeepSeek; it does not convert money into tokens or purchase credit.

The site checks provider funding where available and declines requests that exceed known credits. Your provider account settings remain yours to control. Use a dedicated key and provider-side restrictions where available.

Storage and maintenance

Keys are held in a server secret store or encrypted donor vault. Session and usage snapshots are encrypted, with private older snapshot versions retained for up to 14 days for recovery. Withdrawal disables a donated credential; encrypted accounting records remain for usage protection. Provider services have their own retention policies.

The project owner updates remaining allowances and provider availability during maintenance. Shared funding supports answers and voices; it buys no influence over case evidence or verdicts. For a project issue, use the public repository without including keys or private withdrawal codes.
